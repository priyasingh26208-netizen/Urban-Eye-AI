# 🌆 UrbanEyeAI

### AI-Powered Civic Infrastructure Monitoring & Management Platform

---

## 📖 Overview

UrbanEyeAI is an intelligent civic infrastructure monitoring platform that leverages Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, CCTV surveillance, Satellite Imagery, and Citizen Reports to detect, prioritize, track, predict, and verify civic issues in real time.

Traditional civic complaint systems depend heavily on manual reporting and inspections, which often lead to delayed responses and unresolved issues. UrbanEyeAI transforms this process through proactive monitoring, automated issue detection, predictive analytics, and AI-powered verification.

The platform empowers citizens, government officers, and administrators with a unified ecosystem for smarter urban governance.

---

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Urban areas face numerous infrastructure challenges every day, including:

* Potholes and damaged roads
* Garbage accumulation
* Overflowing waste bins
* Water leakage
* Drainage blockage
* Waterlogging and flooding
* Broken streetlights
* Open manholes
* Illegal dumping

Most existing systems suffer from:

* Delayed issue detection
* Dependence on manual complaints
* Inefficient monitoring
* Lack of prioritization
* Poor transparency
* No predictive capabilities
* Manual verification processes

As cities continue to grow, traditional monitoring approaches become increasingly difficult and expensive to manage.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

UrbanEyeAI introduces a proactive AI-driven civic intelligence platform that continuously monitors urban infrastructure using multiple data sources.

The system combines:

* Citizen Reports
* CCTV Surveillance
* Satellite Imagery
* Historical Complaint Data
* AI Analytics

to automatically detect issues, predict future risks, prioritize actions, route tasks to responsible departments, and verify completed work.

This significantly improves efficiency, accountability, and quality of urban services.

# 🏗️ System Workflow

### Step 1: Multi-Source Detection

Issues are detected through:

* Citizen Reports
* CCTV Cameras
* Satellite Images

### Step 2: AI Classification

Artificial Intelligence identifies:

* Issue Category
* Severity Level
* Impact Assessment

### Step 3: Smart Prioritization

Issues are categorized as:

* Critical
* High Priority
* Medium Priority
* Low Priority

### Step 4: Automatic Department Routing

Issues are automatically assigned to:

* Road Department
* Waste Management Department
* Water Department
* Drainage Department
* Electrical Department

### Step 5: Officer Assignment

Relevant officers receive tasks based on department and location.

### Step 6: Resolution Tracking

The system continuously tracks progress and updates stakeholders.

### Step 7: AI Verification

Before-and-after evidence is analyzed to verify genuine issue resolution.

# ✨ Core Features

## 🧠 AI Image-Based Civic Issue Detection

Citizens can upload photographs of civic issues.

AI automatically detects:

* Potholes
* Road Damage
* Garbage Accumulation
* Overflowing Bins
* Water Leakage
* Broken Streetlights
* Open Manholes
* Drainage Blockages
* Illegal Dumping
* Damaged Footpaths

The system also:

* Estimates severity
* Determines affected area
* Assigns priority
* Suggests responsible department

---

## 📹 CCTV-Based Monitoring

UrbanEyeAI continuously analyzes CCTV feeds using Computer Vision.

Capabilities include:

* Traffic Obstruction Detection
* Flood Detection
* Waterlogging Detection
* Illegal Dumping Detection
* Infrastructure Damage Monitoring

This enables authorities to identify issues even when no complaint is submitted.

---

## 🛰️ Satellite Image Analysis

Satellite imagery is utilized for large-scale urban monitoring.

Applications include:

* Road Condition Assessment
* Waterlogging Analysis
* Infrastructure Degradation Detection
* Area-wide Monitoring

This provides visibility in locations where manual inspections are difficult.

---

## 🔥 AI Civic Hotspot Prediction

UrbanEyeAI predicts future civic issues before they occur.

The prediction engine analyzes:

* Historical Complaints
* Complaint Density
* Geographic Distribution
* Infrastructure Conditions
* Weather Data
* Seasonal Patterns

Example Prediction:

"Sector 21 has a high probability of drainage-related complaints within the next 7 days."

This enables preventive action rather than reactive response.

---

## 🏆 Civic Health Score

Each area receives a Civic Health Index.

Example:

Garbage Management: 71

Water Infrastructure: 85

Drainage System: 63

Street Lighting: 91

Overall Civic Health Score: 78/100

Benefits:

* Area Comparison
* Performance Monitoring
* Resource Planning
* Infrastructure Assessment

## 🤖 AI Resolution Verification

UrbanEyeAI verifies whether an issue has actually been resolved.

The system compares:

Before Image

VS

After Image

and determines:

* Fully Resolved
* Partially Resolved
* Not Resolved

This improves accountability and transparency.
